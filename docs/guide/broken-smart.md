# Broken smart crates

**Broken smart crates** lists smart crates whose rules are missing. Serato then shows “There is a problem with this Smart Crate.” Checking the list writes nothing. Repair puts the saved rules back for the crates you checked.

The note under the heading says **Lists smart crates that lost their rules. Check again writes nothing. Repair puts the saved rules back. Quit Serato first.**

This view covers the whole space.

## Repair

1. Open crateCraft.
2. Click **Health** in the drawer, then **Broken smart crates**.
3. The list names each broken crate, why it is listed, and where the rules were found. A crate with no saved rules stays on the list and cannot be checked.
4. Uncheck any crate you want to leave as it is.
5. Click **Repair**. The page shows the plan. Nothing is written until you confirm.
6. Click **Confirm repair**. Quit Serato first. Only those rules are written. Other crates and the tracks in them stay as they are.

crateCraft reads the rules back. If they are still missing, the snapshot is put back and the page says so.

**Undo repair** puts that snapshot back.

**Check again** reloads the list. Run it again after another program syncs the library. That check does not write.

When no smart crate is missing its rules, the report says **No smart crate is missing its rules.**
